# AI-Photovoltaic-Power-Forecasting
Integrating Panel Soiling, Weather Dynamics, and Solar Irradiance for Ac-curate Photovoltaic Power Forecasting
Abstract
Accurate photovoltaic (PV) power forecasting is critical for grid stability and energy management, yet remains chal-lenging due to the complex interplay of environmental and operational factors. Existing methods predominantly rely on single-modality inputs typically weather data, visual inspection, and fail to capture the complementary in-teractions across multiple sources. In this work, we propose a multimodal deep learning framework that jointly models weather, sunlight, and panel contamination for robust PV power prediction. To enable contamination-aware learning, we introduce CBNU-SolarPower, a novel dataset featuring temporally synchronized weather records and high-resolution PV panel images with carefully annotated soiling labels spanning diverse real-world contamination conditions. Our framework learns modality-specific representations through dedicated encoders and fuses them via cross-modal concatenation to preserve complementary information. Extensive experiments across all modality combinations under five-fold cross-validation show that the full trimodal configuration achieves a mean R² of 93.74, RMSE of 18.66, and MAE of 13.57, outperforming the best single-modality baseline by over 34% in R². Notably, while sunlight serves as the dominant predictive signal (R² = 92.21 standalone), incorporating weather and contamination modalities yields consistent gains in both accuracy and generalization.
